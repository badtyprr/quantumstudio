## Per-word probabilities and known solution count

On page 20 a word in the unmarked class has amplitude α/√(N−s), while a marked word has amplitude β/√s. Squaring the modulus gives each word’s probability. Summing the s marked probabilities gives |β|². This applies to arbitrary normalized complex α and β with both classes nonempty. For pages 27–28, let θ = arcsin √(s/N) with 1 ≤ s ≤ N. The chosen iteration count t = floor(π/(4θ)) puts (2t+1)θ within θ of π/2. Its squared sine is therefore at least cos²θ = 1−s/N. If s/N exceeds one half, t is zero and the success probability equals s/N. Together these cases give the slide’s maximum of the two lower bounds. Since sin θ ≤ θ, t ≤ π/(4θ) ≤ (π/4)√(N/s).
